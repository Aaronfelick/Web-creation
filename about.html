About Us

Trusted partners in global trade.

Delman has been a recognised torchbearer in the freight forwarding and logistics industry since 2010. With a global reach spanning six continents, we take pleasure in providing great services to our clients.

Our charismatic and dynamic team blends youthful exuberance with seasoned professionalism to furnish requisite solutions. Known for specialist knowledge and unmatched responsiveness, we are marshaling to be one of the leading shipping and logistics service providers in the region.

We are certified by the General Civil Aviation Authority, ISO, CASS IATA, and FIATA. With our dedication to top-notch service and unwavering excellence, Delman continues to be one of the leaders in the industry.
